About Me

I am Althea Nicole Sumang, a Deaf creative and a second-year student in Visual Media Arts (VMA) at Benilde. I like creating meaningful and creative work while exploring graphic design, video, drawing, and visual storytelling with a fresh Gen Z perspective.

Studying VMA has helped me grow not only in my skills but also as a person. Through my art, I express what words alone cannot and share my ideas and feelings through visuals.

In this portfolio, you will see projects that show my growth in VMA.
