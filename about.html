About Me

I am Althea Nicole Sumang, a Deaf creative and a second-year student in Visual Media Arts (VMA) at Benilde. I like creating meaningful and creative work while exploring graphic design, video, drawing, and visual storytelling with a fresh Gen Z perspective. Once I find something fun, there’s no going back.

Studying VMA has helped me grow not only in my skills but also as a person. Through my art, I express what words alone cannot and share my ideas and feelings through visuals.

In this portfolio, you will see projects that show my growth in VMA. These include DIGIPOTO, DIGIMAG, DELPRIN, DIGITRA, and BASIVID. Each project helped me learn new skills—like using tools, making better designs, and communicating ideas clearly through visuals.

For now, this portfolio shows my works from different VMA projects. I aim to create work that is simple, honest, and easy to understand, while still making a strong impression.
